### 2.1. Proposal Description

HBCN seek approval to be able to transact synthetic loans on an ongoing basis with relevant hedging derivatives booked from the HBCN MSS balance sheet. These loans may be spot or forward start in nature or have a fixed rate coupon, or may be in a currency different to our natural funding currencies requiring a derivative hedge and therefore aligned to being booked in MSS due to the ongoing risk management requirements

The product caters to situations where a client requires a Loan in a particular currency or format that would not be directly provided by Markets Treasury and requires the use of a derivative hedges (IRS / CCS / Interest rate or FX Options) so as to generate a cheaper funding cost under certain market window to benefit end client. However, when client choose to early repay loan, the associated hedging derivative needs early termination, therefore, such termination should be borne by client as well.

### 2.5.	Overall Transaction Flow

#### Synthetic Loan Sample

1. MSS to borrow USD funding from MKTY (Trade #1)
2. MSS to trade CCS with interbank CP to convert USD into CNY (Trade #2)
3. MSS to lend CNY synthetic loan to customer group who further book against customer in HUB (Trade #3/#4)
4. Client can enter such loan with lower rate at the cost of bearing unwind cost upon early withdraw event given MSS need to unwind CCS accordingly.

Note: Trade #3- *RTL Loan will be booked in XFOS in an MSS book – FVTPL Accounting & Regulatory Banking Book treatment.
The discount curve for the Loan will be discussed and agreed with product Control prior to dealing. This will be derived from the underlying synthetic funding rate (CCS, NDS or IRS) in the currency of the Loan and the credit spread of the borrower. The synthetic funding rate will be updated on a daily basis. The credit spread will be updated periodically by FO, and validated every month-end in the valuation committee by Product control. Please see below for further description of the valuation process.

FO will work with PC to see if an alternate booking procedure can be proposed, especially to handle multiple Loans in the same currency to different borrowers.

#### Scenario 1: Client repay at maturity

- Internal loan (Trade #1): Normal mature
- External CCS (Trade #2): GMO to process as per BAU
- *RTL Loan (Trade #3): Normal mature
- HUB client loan (Trade #4): Credit OPS to process as per BAU

#### Scenario 2: Client early repay

- Internal loan (Trade #1): Early terminate with cost.
- External CCS (Trade #2): Early terminate with cost. GMO to handle cost settlement against external CP.
- *RTL Loan (Trade #3): Early terminate. FO to book fee swap for unwind cost charge.
- HUB client loan (Trade #4): Credit OPS to process early repayment. GMO to pass unwind cost to credit OPS to post accounting entry between customer account and suspense account.
